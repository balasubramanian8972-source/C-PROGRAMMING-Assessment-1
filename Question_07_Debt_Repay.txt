QUESTION 7: DEBT REPAY

Question:
Alice borrows a principal amount for a number of years at a given annual interest rate. Calculate the interest, total amount, a 2% discount on the interest, and the final settlement. Print all rupee values to two decimal places.

Answer (C Program):
#include <stdio.h>

int main() {
    float principal, rate, years;
    float interest, amount, discount, final_amount;

    scanf("%f", &principal);
    scanf("%f", &rate);
    scanf("%f", &years);

    interest = principal * rate * years / 100;
    amount = principal + interest;
    discount = interest * 2 / 100;
    final_amount = amount - discount;

    printf("%.2f\n", interest);
    printf("%.2f\n", amount);
    printf("%.2f\n", discount);
    printf("%.2f\n", final_amount);

    return 0;
}

Sample Input:
100
1
10

Sample Output:
10.00
110.00
0.20
109.80
